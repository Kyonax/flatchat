Items are made for the three standard bodies (rule `body-kits`). Each carries one fitted mesh per body it fits, refitted between the bodies while it is made (shared topology), never at runtime (dp-001; Roblox's cage-fitting patents, cl-004). It fills one fixed slot and hides whole regions of the body (dp-002). An item from another program (FBX, OBJ, DAE, `.blend`) takes the road in rule `model-formats` (S5.08), its declaration given at upload when the file keeps none. Creators' guide: `docs/items.md`.

## The Rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| The slot `effect` (S6.01) is worn at a point like `attachment` (`POINT_SLOTS`), with a `scale`; model uploads list `MODEL_SLOTS`, which leave it out | An effect is checked by its dry run (rule `effects`), not as a mesh |
| The slot `overlay` (S6.02) is a PNG worn at a screen point (`hud`), keyed in outfits by that point (`wornKey`), four at most; not a point slot, not a model slot | An overlay is an image on the screen (rule `overlays`), never a mesh on the body |
| Regions are cut where garments end: the torso is `chest`, `belly` and `hips`, never one region | Hiding is by whole region: a tee hiding a torso that included the pelvis left a hole at the hips, and one not hiding it let a seated thigh come through its front |
| A fit's skeleton is compared with the kit's in the bind pose (`invert(inverseBind)` on both), at the trunk and legs only (`SHAPE_JOINTS`) | The kits' VRMs rest in a T-pose with legs straightened by the VRM add-on, but are bound as the `.blend` stands, as a creator's fit is; arms differ between A-pose and T-pose. A fit made on another body is off by 5 to 10 cm, the tee by 0 |
| The clipping check casts a ray along each visible skin point's normal: cloth facing the skin's way within `CLIP_REACH` (10 cm) outside covers it, such cloth within `CLIP_DEPTH` (3 cm) inside is skin out through it by that depth; at rest the covered (or already through) points are the garment's, away from its openings (`EDGE_ZONE` 3 cm), and in each pose they must stay within `CLIP_TOLERANCE` (3 mm) | The nearest cloth point read a folded cuff's rim, a collar's band or a lining (all facing the body) as skin through cloth, and a hand past a cuff as clipping; a 2.5 cm reach missed a loose sleeve and a boxy jacket's side 6 to 10 cm out |
| Skin the body hides itself is not counted: a fold (the body's own surface within `FOLD_REACH` in front of it) or a crossing (another part's skin just under it, facing out) | A deep seat folds the thighs into the belly and an arm held down covers the chest's side; no garment can cover the body's own overlap |
| Refuse at more than 10 points out and over 5% of those near, or any point 25 mm out; fewer is a warning | A share alone refused the good tee on 2 of 2 points; a garment that hides everything it covers honestly checks 0 points |
| Pose with `poseMatrices` (stride.ts) and put the rest world back for skinning: world = (posed × rest⁻¹) × rest, then × inverse bind | The impostor's stride code gives skinning matrices for world-space rest positions; a fit's vertices are in its bind space |
| Weight cloth from the skin straight under it (a ray along the cloth's inward normal; the nearest point only where nothing is under it): `weightsFromSkin` | MPFB weights clothes from its helper geometry, which bends otherwise (shirts 4 to 10 mm into the bust, jeans through the knee when seated); the nearest skin to a loose cuff is the thumb's base, not the wrist it covers |
| Hide a region only where every body has it wholly under the cloth (`coverageOf` 1) or in MakeHuman's delete group (99.5 %), and a side only with its pair: `wardrobeHides` | One declaration serves three bodies: a region one body leaves 0.7 % uncovered opens a hole on it; MakeHuman's delete groups are conservative (jeans delete half the lower leg yet cover all of it), so cover is measured too |
| Push cloth out only where skin still comes through, fading over neighbours facing the same way, `PUSH_LIMIT` (12 mm) in all: `pushOut` | Pushing without a limit "fixed" pose-only clipping by moving cloth past the ray's reach, 10 to 30 cm out; the limit leaves such a garment failing honestly |
| Check the wardrobe at both ends of the sliders too (`WARDROBE_SHAPES`), with the limbs stretched as the client stretches them | Before the stretch (segments.ts in the client), a forearm at 80 % slid into the upper arm and its hand sank into the forearm, so every long sleeve's cuff folded through the wrist; weight smoothing and harmonic weights for bridging cloth (a skirt's middle) both made clipping worse |
| Look at every garment's texture before taking it: MakeHuman's tees carry its logo (painted out of the white one), shoes05 and shoes06 are photographed adidas and Nike sneakers | CC0 waives copyright, not trademark |
| To look at a multi-fit item in Blender, split it per body first (`convertItem`'s fit files) | Blender's glTF importer puts all three fits on one armature (their joints share names) and adds an Icosphere bone shape |
| Change the item format or the checks: bump `ITEM_CONVERTER_VERSION`; regions or bones: `packages/content` first, then `generate.py`'s `REGIONS` and `run.mts`, then `make-test-item.ts` | Every program reads the same rules; the kits' `mask_*` groups and the tee must match them |

## Skin Layers (S5.03)

`packages/content/src/layers.ts`: `compositeSkin(layers, {requireUnderwear})` composites RGBA images in the kits' UV space, the order fixed by `LAYER_KINDS` (skin, tattoo, makeup, underwear on top). Blending is `normal` or `multiply` at an opacity, in integer arithmetic, so the bytes are the same on every machine; an optional ORM image per layer is composited beside the colour (the PBR awareness dp-002 asks for; normal maps are not merged yet). It throws `LayerError` for a stack with no skin, with no underwear (OQ-11, unless `requireUnderwear: false`), or with a size that differs from the skin's. The kits write their layers apart (`generate.py` `underwear()`: `<name>-skin.png`, `<name>-underwear.png`). `tools/converter/test/layers.test.ts` composites them and compares the result with each kit VRM's own texture: zero pixels differ.

## Attachment Points (S5.04)

`packages/content/src/attach.ts`:

- **`ATTACH_POINTS`** has twelve points, each a VRM humanoid bone plus an offset given as a share of the height, in the rest frame (+Z forward). The kits' `attach_*` markers are these points for a 1.7 m body.
- **`attachBone`** falls back up the chain for VRM's optional bones (upperChest to chest to spine, neck to head).
- **`attachMatrix(point, boneRest, boneNow, height, adjust)`** = boneNow × boneRest⁻¹ × (the place at rest, plus the adjustment), so a prop follows its bone.
- **`fitAdjustment`** clamps a player's offset (10 cm), turn (45° per axis) and size (0.8 to 1.25); whatever comes from the network is clamped.

An attachment item is `slot: attachment` with `attach`: one rigid mesh, no skin, worn on any avatar (`convertProp` in item/convert.ts, `checkItemProp`, `manifest.prop`). `tools/converter/test/attach.test.ts` checks that the computed points land within 5 cm of every kit marker, that every point finds a bone and lands on the free-shape robot and the VRM reference avatar, and that a prop converts while a skinned one or one with no point is refused.

## Outfits (S5.05)

| Part | Where |
|---|---|
| Wearable together | `outfitProblems` (packages/content item.ts): one item to a slot, one prop to a point, a `full` item with no `top` or `bottom` beside it. The client keeps a toggle within it (the new item takes the place of the one it conflicts with); the core refuses a saved outfit that breaks it |
| A prop's adjustment in the protocol's units | `WornAdjust`, `WORN_LIMITS` (10 cm, 45 degrees, 80 to 125 percent, from `ADJUST_LIMITS`), `fitWornAdjust` (round and clamp: the world's `fitOutfit`), `wornAdjustment` (to metres and radians for `attachMatrix`) in attach.ts |
| In the world | Protocol version 9: the `outfit` message and each roster player's `outfit` (rule `protocol-and-codecs`); the world fits it and takes one change a second |
| Uploads, saved outfits, the standard bodies | The core (rule `core-service`: `src/items.ts`, `item-cli.ts` in the sandbox with `kits/bodies/`, `POST /api/v1/avatars/kit`, an avatar of a kit's exact bytes marked `kit`) |
| A sample prop | `sampleProp(declare, skinned?)` in `tools/converter/src/item/samples.ts` (`@flatchat/converter/item-samples`), for the converter's and the core's tests |

The client wears them (S5.05c, rule `client-app`): a fit on a standard body by bone names with its own inverse bind matrices, the regions it hides taken off the body, props at their points on any avatar, the outfits quick menu (O), the worn outfit kept by account in the settings.

## Not Yet

Skin layers in the world: no item kind carries a tattoo or makeup layer yet, so nothing calls `compositeSkin` at an outfit change (the kits' textures already hold the underwear); the starter wardrobe has none either. A skirt or low-waisted leggings that hold when seated need more than skinning (their own bones, or a seated pose of their own). Items on impostors (an impostor-sized fit per item) are not made: past LOD2 a player shows the body alone. The gallery and licence fields are S5.06.
